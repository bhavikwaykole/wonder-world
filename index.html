THE WORLD IS WAITING

Places worth travelling for

From peaceful beaches to beautiful mountains and vibrant cities, get inspired for your next adventure.

ITALY · EUROPE

Amalfi Coast

Discover colourful seaside villages, beautiful beaches and winding coastal roads. Enjoy fresh Italian food and unforgettable sea views.

Best time: April – June, September

Must see: Positano, Amalfi, Ravello

Travel tip: Use local ferries to explore the coast.

SWITZERLAND · EUROPE

Swiss Alps

Experience snow-capped peaks, clear lakes and charming mountain villages. It is a perfect destination for nature lovers and hikers.

Best time: June – September

Must see: Zermatt, Interlaken, Jungfrau

Travel tip: Check weather conditions before hiking.

JAPAN · ASIA

Tokyo

Explore a city where modern skyscrapers meet traditional temples. Enjoy exciting neighbourhoods, delicious food and unique culture.

Best time: March – May, October – November

Must see: Shibuya, Senso-ji, Tokyo Tower

Travel tip: Get a rechargeable transit card for trains.
